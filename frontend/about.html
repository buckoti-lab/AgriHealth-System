ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Poject Title

AI-Powered System for Detecting and Classifying Vegetable Diseases

Project Description
This system uses Artificial Intelligence to detect and classify diseases affecting vegetable crops. Farmers can upload a photo of a sick leaf and receive instant diagnosis with treatment recommendations.
Key features
  • Upload leaf image for instant diagnosis
  • Detects 20 diseases across 5 vegetable crops
  • Provides treatment recommendations
  • Saves diagnosis history
